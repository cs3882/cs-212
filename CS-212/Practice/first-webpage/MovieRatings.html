My Favorite Movies

The top 3

The 13th Warrior

There is always something fun about a B budget movie that snags an A lister before they "make it".
In this movie we have a cast of overly handsome, questionably dirty... Vikings maybe? Called to defend a
weakened kingdom against the "Eaters of Flesh".

Starring a young Antonio Banderas as the exiled Arabian poet prophecied to make all the difference in
this battle.
Complete with his Spanish accent. What more could you ask for?


Practical Magic

Genuinely so excited to see the new one this weekend. This movie is a fun roller coaster teaching the
connection of sisters is more than any curse could ever withstand and asking is love the most powerful
magic of all?


The Fifth Element

This movie is such a great example of how to weave disparate storylines together in the most fun way
possible. At at least two points you see something like five separate storylines converging in one scene.
Featuring young Mila Jovovovich and Bruce Willis, this movie is a fun romp through a futuristic world where
the fate of the universe rests in the hands of a disillusioned war hero.

Plus the greatest opera song in any movie ever. End of.
